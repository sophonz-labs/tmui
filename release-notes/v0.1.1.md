tmui installs with one line now:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://dl.sophonz.io/tmui/install.sh | sh
```

The installer picks the build for your OS and CPU, checks it against the release's SHA-256 sums, and puts `tmui` in `~/.local/bin` (`TMUI_INSTALL_DIR` to change it, `TMUI_VERSION` to pin a release). Nothing else changed since v0.1.0.
